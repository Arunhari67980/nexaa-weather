# Weather App - User Guide

## 🎯 Getting Started

### First Launch
1. Enter any city name in the search bar (e.g., "London", "New York", "Tokyo")
2. Press Enter or click a suggestion
3. View current weather, forecast, and detailed information

---

## 🔍 Main Features

### Real-Time Search
- **Type a city name** in the search bar
- **Autocomplete** suggestions appear as you type
- **Click a suggestion** or press Enter to search
- Search results are **automatically saved** to recent searches

### Current Weather Display
**Left Side** (Large Card):
- Current temperature with large gradient display
- Weather condition with emoji (☀️, 🌧️, ⛈️, ❄️, etc.)
- Feels-like temperature
- 6 key metrics grid:
  - 💧 Humidity
  - 💨 Wind Speed
  - 📊 Pressure
  - 👁️ Visibility
  - ☁️ Cloud Cover
  - ☀️ UV Index
- Min/Max temperatures

### Temperature Trends Chart
- **5-day forecast visualization**
- Blue line = Actual temperature
- Orange dashed line = Feels-like temperature
- **Hover over chart** for exact values
- Daily forecast details below

### Enhanced Details (Right Sidebar)
**Sun Times**
- 🌅 Sunrise time (HH:MM format)
- 🌆 Sunset time (HH:MM format)

**Wind Direction**
- Visual compass with emoji arrows (↓↙←↖↑↗→↘)
- Cardinal direction (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW)
- Wind speed

**Activity Suggestions**
- 5 weather-appropriate activities
- Examples:
  - ☀️ Sunny → Running, Biking, Beach
  - 🌧️ Rainy → Movies, Reading, Gaming
  - ❄️ Snowy → Skiing, Ice Skating
  - 🔥 Hot → Swimming, Ice Cream

**Outfit Recommendations**
- 🧥 Top clothing (Coat, Jacket, T-shirt, etc.)
- 👖 Bottom clothing (Jeans, Shorts, etc.)
- 🧣 Accessories (Hat, Sunglasses, Umbrella, etc.)
- 💬 Personalized advice ("Bring an umbrella!")

**Quick Stats**
- 👁️ Visibility
- 💧 Humidity
- 📊 Pressure
- ☁️ Cloudiness

### Air Quality Index (AQI)
Located in the right sidebar:
- **AQI Level**: Good (😊) → Fair (🙂) → Moderate (😐) → Poor (😷) → Very Poor (😫)
- **Health Recommendations**: Personalized guidance based on AQI
- **Pollutant Details** (6 metrics):
  - PM2.5 (Fine particulates)
  - PM10 (Coarse particulates)
  - NO₂ (Nitrogen dioxide)
  - O₃ (Ozone)
  - SO₂ (Sulfur dioxide)
  - CO (Carbon monoxide)
- **Sensitive Groups Warning** for children, elderly, people with respiratory/heart conditions

### Hourly Forecast
- **Next 8 hours** of weather
- Horizontal carousel (scroll right for more)
- Each hour shows:
  - 🕐 Time
  - Weather emoji
  - Temperature
  - Precipitation probability

### Weekly Forecast
- **7-day forecast** grid
- Each day shows:
  - 📅 Date
  - ☁️ Condition
  - 🌡️ Min/Max temps
  - 💨 Wind info
  - Click to expand details

## ⭐ Favorites & History

### Save Favorite Cities
1. **Click the Star (⭐)** button in the header
2. Star turns **yellow** when favorited
3. Favorited cities appear in the **Favorites section** (right sidebar)

### View Recent Searches
**Right Sidebar** shows:
- **⭐ Favorites** (saved cities)
- **🕐 Recent** (last 10 searches)
- **Delete button** appears on hover
- Click any city to instantly load its weather

## ⚙️ Settings & Preferences

### Open Settings
Click the **Settings icon (⚙️)** in the header

### General Tab
- **Temperature Unit**: Toggle between °C and °F
  - Affects all displayed temperatures
  - Saved automatically
- **Default View**: Choose default layout (Cards or Map)

### Display Tab
- **Theme**: Auto / Light / Dark
  - Auto: Follows system preference
  - Light: Bright theme
  - Dark: Dark theme (current)
- **Display Options** (checkboxes):
  - Wind speed
  - Humidity
  - Pressure

### Alerts Tab
- **Alert Banner**: Toggle to show/hide alerts
- **Alert Types**:
  - ⚠️ Severe Weather
  - 💨 High Winds
  - 🌧️ Heavy Rain
  - ❄️ Snow Warning

## 🔄 Refresh & Comparison

### Refresh Weather
- Click the **Refresh button (🔄)** in the header
- Shows spinner while updating
- Fetches latest data from OpenWeatherMap

### Compare Two Cities
1. Click the **Comparison button (📊)** in the header
2. Enter second city name
3. Click **Compare**
4. See side-by-side metrics:
   - 🌡️ Temperature comparison
   - 💨 Wind speed comparison
   - 💧 Humidity comparison
   - 👁️ Visibility comparison
   - 📊 Pressure comparison
   - ☁️ Cloud cover comparison
5. Summary section shows which city is warmer/windier/more humid

---

## 🗺️ Map View

### Toggle Map View
1. Click the **Map button (📍)** in the header
2. Weather information displayed on an interactive map
3. Click Map button again to return to cards view

## 📊 Temperature Unit Toggle

### Quick Unit Switch
1. Click the **Unit button (°C or °F)** in the header
2. All temperatures instantly convert

## 📤 Export & Share

### Export Weather as CSV
1. In **Enhanced Details** section, click **Download button (📥)**
2. CSV file downloads with:
   - Date and time
   - Current temperature
   - Feels-like temperature
   - Humidity
   - Wind speed
   - Pressure
   - Weather condition

### Share Weather
1. In **Enhanced Details** section, click **Share button (🔗)**
2. Choose sharing method:
   - Copy to clipboard
   - Open native share (mobile)
   - Share to social media

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why aren't all pollutants showing in AQI?**
A: OpenWeatherMap free tier shows available pollutants for your location.

**Q: Can I share without native share API?**
A: Yes! Falls back to clipboard copy for older browsers.

**Q: Will my data sync across devices?**
A: No, stored locally. You'll need to save favorites again on other devices.

**Q: Is there an offline mode?**
A: Not yet, but it's planned as a future feature.

**Q: Can I get weather alerts?**
A: Yes! Enable alerts in Settings → Alerts tab.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Not Finding City
- Check spelling
- Try "City, Country" format
- Verify city exists in OpenWeatherMap database

### Data Not Updating
- Click refresh button (🔄)
- Check internet connection
- Try a different city

### Animations Laggy
- Disable heavy animations in settings (if available)
- Check device performance
- Try closing other apps

### Settings Not Saving
- Check if browser allows localStorage
- Clear cache and try again
- Check storage quota
